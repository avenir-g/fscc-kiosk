# Design System

## Colors
- Black: #1C1C1C
- White: #FFFFFF
- Accent: #0E85B5

## Typography
- Montserrat for body text
- Jost Extra Black for selected titles only
- keep titles strong and readable from a distance

## Layout
- generous stage padding
- independent card gap separation
- large readable slide titles
- overview cards keep a calm editorial rhythm

## Surfaces
- automatic
- media
- light
- dark
- accent

## Motion
- crossfade transition
- subtle text motion
- default slide motion: zoom + pan
- welcome slide can override with a cinematic treatment

## Controls
- readable buttons, tags, and overlays
- strong contrast on dark and light surfaces
